Privacy Policy

Sowaka (the “app”) · Last updated 10 September 2026

Sowaka is a workplace HR app. Your employer licenses it to manage attendance, leave, overtime, reimbursements, performance reviews and internal communication. Your account is created by your employer’s HR team and belongs to them; we process your information on their behalf as a data processor, and they decide what is collected and how long it is kept.

What we collect

CategoryWhat it includesWhy
Identity & contact Name, work email, employee ID, department, designation, reporting manager, work location, date of joining, date of birth, profile photo To sign you in and show you to colleagues in the directory and org chart
Attendance Punch-in and punch-out times, correction requests and their approvals To record hours worked and process corrections
Leave & overtime Requests, dates, reasons you type, balances, manager decisions and notes To run the leave and overtime process
Reimbursements Claim amounts, expense dates, notes and receipt images you upload To process expense claims
Performance Review scores, written feedback, recognition nominations To run your employer’s review cycle
Connect posts Posts, comments, reactions, poll responses, photos and videos you upload, and people you tag To run the internal feed
Device & technical Push notification token, app version, device model and OS version, and server logs of requests (including IP address) To deliver notifications, and to keep the service secure and working

We do not collect precise location, contacts, calendar, health data, or anything from other apps on your device. The app requests microphone access only when you use voice input to dictate feedback; audio is converted to text on your device by the operating system and the recording is not sent to us or stored.

How it is used

  • To provide the features above to you and your employer.
  • To send you notifications about things that concern you — a leave decision, a review, a request awaiting you — by push and by email.
  • To keep the service secure, diagnose faults and prevent misuse.

We do not sell your personal data. We do not use it for advertising, and we do not profile you for any purpose outside the features described here.

Who can see it

  • Your colleagues see your name, photo, designation, team and anything you post on Connect.
  • Your manager sees your attendance, leave, overtime and reimbursement requests, and writes your reviews.
  • Your HR team sees the above across the organisation.
  • Service providers we use to run the app: cloud hosting and database (Amazon Web Services, MongoDB Atlas), push notifications (Google Firebase Cloud Messaging), and email delivery. They process data only to provide those services to us.
  • Nobody else, unless the law requires it.

Where it is kept, and for how long

Data is stored on servers operated by our cloud providers. Your employer sets retention; as a rule, your records are kept while your account is active and for as long afterwards as employment law requires them to be — typically attendance and payroll-related records for the statutory period.

Deleting your account and data

Your account is your employer’s, so it is normally closed when you leave. To have it closed and your personal data deleted sooner:

  • Email your HR team, copying privacy@getsowaka.com, from your work address.
  • We acknowledge within 7 working days and delete within 30 days of your employer confirming the request.
  • Records your employer must keep by law — attendance and payroll history — are retained for the statutory period and then deleted.
  • You can request a copy of your data the same way.

In the app, the same instructions are under Actions ’ Policies ’ Your data.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your personal data, and to object to or restrict certain processing. Because your employer controls this data, please raise these requests with your HR team; we will support them in meeting the request. You can also write to us directly at the address below.

Security

Access requires a one-time code sent to your work email. Data is encrypted in transit. Access within the app follows your role — your manager and HR see what their role allows, colleagues see only what is shared with everyone.

Children

Sowaka is a workplace tool and is not directed at anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, we will update the date at the top and notify your employer, who will inform staff.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: privacy@getsowaka.com. For anything about your own records, your HR team is the fastest route.
