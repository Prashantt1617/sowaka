Privacy Policy

Sowaka · Android package com.sowaka.connect · iOS com.tanvijadhav.sowaka
Effective 10 September 2026 · Last updated 10 September 2026

Sowaka is a human resource management system (HRMS) — a workplace app licensed to employers to run attendance, leave, overtime, reimbursements, performance reviews and internal communication for their staff. It is not a consumer product and is not available to the general public. This policy explains what Sowaka collects, who sees it, how long it is kept, and how an account and its data are deleted.

Who is responsible for your data

Your employer decides what is collected in Sowaka and why; they are the data controller. Sowaka operates the app and processes that information on their instructions as the data processor. Requests about your own records are usually fastest through your HR team, and you may also write to us directly at the address at the end of this policy.

How your account is created

You cannot create a Sowaka account yourself. There is no public sign-up, and the app cannot be used without an employer.

  • Your employer's HR team creates your account from their own employment records, entering your name, work email address, employee ID, department, designation, reporting manager, work location and joining date.
  • You sign in with your work email address only. Sowaka emails you a one-time six-digit code; there is no password to set or remember, and we do not use social sign-in.
  • Your employer decides who has an account, and what each person can see — an employee, a manager and an HR administrator see different things.
  • Adding your profile photo, and everything you post on the Connect feed, is voluntary.

What Sowaka does not collect

No precise or background location, no contacts, no calendar, no SMS or call logs, no health or financial account data, no biometric identifiers, and nothing from other apps on your device. Sowaka contains no advertising and no third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

Microphone: the app asks for microphone access only when you choose voice input to dictate feedback. Speech is converted to text by your device's operating system; the audio is not sent to us, and no recording is stored.

How it is used

  • To provide the HR features described above to you and your employer.
  • To notify you about things that concern you — a leave decision, a review shared with you, a request awaiting you — by push notification, in-app message and email.
  • To keep the service secure, diagnose faults, and prevent misuse.

Sowaka does not sell personal data, does not share it with data brokers, and does not use it for advertising or for any purpose beyond the features described here.

Who it is shared with

  • Colleagues at your employer — your name, photo, designation, team, and anything you post on Connect.
  • Your manager — your attendance, leave, overtime and reimbursement requests, and your performance reviews.
  • Your HR administrators — the above, across the organisation.
  • Service providers that run the app on our behalf, under contract and only for that purpose:
    • Amazon Web Services — application hosting and file storage
    • MongoDB Atlas — database hosting
    • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging — push notification delivery
    • Our email provider — one-time sign-in codes and notification email
  • Nobody else, unless we are required to by law, or to protect the rights and safety of users.

Data is processed on servers operated by those providers. Where information is transferred across borders, it is protected by the contractual safeguards those providers offer.

How it is protected

  • Access requires a one-time code sent to your work email; there are no reusable passwords.
  • All traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted in transit using HTTPS.
  • Data at rest is stored on encrypted infrastructure at our hosting providers.
  • Access inside the app follows your role — colleagues see only what is shared with everyone, managers see their own team, HR sees the organisation.
  • Sowaka staff access production data only when needed to operate or support the service.

How long it is kept

Your records are kept while your account is active, and afterwards for as long as your employer must retain them — employment, attendance and payroll records are generally kept for the statutory period that applies to your employer, and are then deleted. Server logs are kept for a short operational period and then discarded. Your employer sets retention within these limits.

How your account and data are deleted

Because accounts are created by your employer, they are also closed by your employer — normally when you leave the organisation. You can also ask for your account and personal data to be deleted at any time:

How to request deletion

  • In the app: open Profile and tap “Sowaka data and privacy policy” at the bottom, which explains the request and the address to write to.
  • By email: write to privacy@getsowaka.com from your work address, copying your HR team. Say that you want your Sowaka account and data deleted.

What happens next

  • We acknowledge your request within 7 working days.
  • Because your employer controls the records, we confirm the request with them, and then delete within 30 days.
  • Deleted: your account and sign-in access, profile and photo, Connect posts, comments and uploads, reimbursement receipts, performance reviews and written feedback, notification history and your device's push token.
  • Retained: attendance and payroll-related records that your employer is legally required to keep, for the statutory retention period, after which they are deleted. These are kept for legal compliance only and are not used for anything else.

You can request a copy of your data by the same route, and we will provide it in a machine-readable form.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, export or delete your personal data, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to complain to a data protection authority. Since your employer controls this data, please raise these requests with your HR team; we support them in meeting the request, and you can also contact us directly.

Children

Sowaka is a workplace tool intended for employed adults. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a way that materially affects you, we will update the date at the top of this page and notify your employer, who will inform their staff. Continued use of the app after a change means the updated policy applies.
