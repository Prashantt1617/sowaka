Delete your account and data

Sowaka · Last updated 10 September 2026

Request deletion

Email us from the work address your Sowaka account uses, and say that you want your account and data deleted. You do not need the app installed.

Email support@getsowaka.com

Or write to support@getsowaka.com with your full name, work email, employer and employee ID. Please copy your HR team, who confirm the request.

In the app

You can also start from the app: open Profile, scroll to the bottom and tap “Sowaka data and privacy policy”, which explains the request and where to send it.

Why we ask your employer first

Sowaka is an HRMS. Your account is created by your employer’s HR team from their employment records, not by you, and the records in it belong to them. We confirm a deletion request with them before acting on it, so that nobody else can have your account removed and so your employer can meet the record-keeping obligations they are under.

What happens, and when

  1. We acknowledge your request within 7 working days.
  2. We confirm it with your employer’s HR team.
  3. We delete within 30 days of that confirmation, and write to tell you it is done.

What is deleted, and what is kept

DeletedKept, and why
Your account and sign-in access; profile details and photo; Connect posts, comments, reactions and uploads; reimbursement claims and receipt images; performance reviews and written feedback; leave and overtime requests; notification history; your device’s push token.

These are erased — not deactivated, hidden or archived. 		Attendance and payroll-related records that your employer is legally required to retain, for the statutory period that applies to them, after which they are deleted. These are kept for legal compliance only and are not used for anything else.

Server logs, which are kept for a short operational period for security and fraud prevention, and then discarded.

A copy of your data

You can request a copy of your data by the same route, and we will provide it in a machine-readable form.
