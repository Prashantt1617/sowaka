Internet — Home broadband and mobile data.
- Capped at ₹1,500 per claim.
- Can be claimed up to 30 days after the expense.

Meals — Meals while travelling or working late.
- Capped at ₹2,000 per claim.
- Can be claimed up to 30 days after the expense.

Other — Anything the categories above do not cover.
- No cap per claim.
- Can be claimed up to 30 days after the expense.

Travel — Cabs, flights, trains and mileage for work travel.
- Capped at ₹25,000 per claim.
- Can be claimed up to 30 days after the expense.
