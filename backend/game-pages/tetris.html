How to play

  1. 1Turn and moveTap the well to turn the piece. Swipe left or right to slide it.
  2. 2Clear linesFill a row from wall to wall and it clears. Four rows at once is a Tetris, worth the most.
  3. 3Drop and holdSwipe down to drop faster, flick down to drop at once, swipe up to keep a piece for later. Every 10 lines the blocks fall faster.

Keyboard: ← → move · ↑ or X turn · Z turn back · ↓ faster · Space drop · C hold · P pause
